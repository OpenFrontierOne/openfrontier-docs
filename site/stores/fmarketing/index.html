FreeMarketingStore / ProMarketingStore (Planned)¶

Status: PLANNING¶

What Marketing Is (the full scope)¶

Marketing is NOT design. Marketing is strategy + distribution + measurement. It's everything AFTER you have your brand and visuals ready.

The complete marketing toolkit:¶

Campaigns & Planning¶

  • Campaign planner (goal → audience → channels → timeline → budget)
  • Content calendar (visual drag-and-drop editorial schedule)
  • Launch planning (pre-launch → launch → post-launch checklists)
  • Product launch frameworks (Hunt, Show HN, Reddit, email)
  • Seasonal campaign templates (Black Friday, New Year, etc)

Social Media Marketing (SMM)¶

  • Social post generator (platform-specific copy + formatting)
  • Hashtag research (trending, niche, competitor analysis)
  • Best-time-to-post analyzer (by platform, by audience timezone)
  • Social media audit tool (analyze any account's performance)
  • Content repurposing (blog → tweets → carousel → video script)
  • Engagement rate calculator
  • Bio generator (platform-specific bios)

Advertising¶

  • Ad copy generator (Facebook Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads)
  • Ad creative tester (preview how ads look on each platform)
  • Budget calculator (CPC/CPM/CPA modeling)
  • ROAS predictor
  • Audience targeting suggestions
  • Landing page builder for ads
  • UTM parameter generator

Analytics & Measurement¶

  • Dashboard builder (connect data sources, visualize KPIs)
  • Funnel visualizer (awareness → interest → desire → action)
  • Cohort analysis (retention, churn visualization)
  • Attribution modeling (which channel drives conversions)
  • ROI calculator per channel
  • Report generator (weekly/monthly marketing reports)

A/B Testing & Optimization¶

  • Headline A/B tester (two variants → predict winner)
  • Landing page split tester
  • Email subject line tester
  • CTA button optimizer (color, text, placement)
  • Sample size calculator (statistical significance)
  • Multivariate test designer

User Research & Focus Groups¶

  • Survey builder (NPS, CSAT, custom)
  • User interview script generator
  • Persona builder (demographics → psychographics → pain points)
  • Customer journey mapper (touchpoints, emotions, opportunities)
  • Focus group discussion guide generator
  • Competitive positioning matrix

Email Marketing¶

  • Email sequence builder (visual drip designer)
  • Subject line generator + A/B predictor
  • Newsletter template builder
  • Segmentation strategy planner
  • Deliverability checker
  • Unsubscribe rate analyzer

SEO & Content Marketing¶

  • Keyword research tool
  • Content brief generator (keyword → full brief with outline)
  • SEO audit tool (any URL → issues + fixes)
  • Backlink opportunity finder
  • Content gap analyzer (your site vs competitor)
  • Title/meta description optimizer
  • Internal linking suggester

PR & Outreach¶

  • Press release generator
  • Media list builder (journalists by beat)
  • Pitch email templates
  • Influencer outreach templates
  • Partnership proposal generator
  • Event marketing planner

Free vs Pro Split¶

Free (browser tools, no signup)¶

ALL planning and generation tools: - Campaign planner, content calendar, persona builder - Ad copy generator, headline tester, subject line tester - SEO audit, keyword suggestions, meta generator - Survey builder, journey mapper, funnel visualizer - UTM generator, ROI calculator, sample size calculator - Social post generator, hashtag research, bio generator

Pro ($9/mo — execution + data)¶

Tools that need real data or API connections: - Actually post to X/LinkedIn/Instagram/Facebook (OAuth) - Real analytics from connected accounts - A/B test runner (hosts landing pages, tracks results) - Email sending (Mailgun/SES integration) - Scheduled posting with cron - Autonomous campaign agent (AI generates → schedules → measures → adapts) - Team workspaces (agency model — manage multiple clients) - White-label reports (your branding on client reports)

Naming¶

  • FreeMarketingStore / ProMarketingStore
  • Abbreviation: FMtS / PMtS (or just "marketing")
  • Domain: freemarketingstore.online / promarketingstore.online
  • Accent: TBD (something energetic — orange? electric blue?)
  • Theme: Light (marketing people expect dashboards, not terminals)
