## What Marketing Is (the full scope)

Marketing is NOT design. Marketing is strategy + distribution + measurement. It's everything AFTER you have your brand and visuals ready.

### The complete marketing toolkit:

#### Campaigns & Planning
- Campaign planner (goal → audience → channels → timeline → budget)
- Content calendar (visual drag-and-drop editorial schedule)
- Launch planning (pre-launch → launch → post-launch checklists)
- Product launch frameworks (Hunt, Show HN, Reddit, email)
- Seasonal campaign templates (Black Friday, New Year, etc)

#### Social Media Marketing (SMM)
- Social post generator (platform-specific copy + formatting)
- Hashtag research (trending, niche, competitor analysis)
- Best-time-to-post analyzer (by platform, by audience timezone)
- Social media audit tool (analyze any account's performance)
- Content repurposing (blog → tweets → carousel → video script)
- Engagement rate calculator
- Bio generator (platform-specific bios)

#### Advertising
- Ad copy generator (Facebook Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads)
- Ad creative tester (preview how ads look on each platform)
- Budget calculator (CPC/CPM/CPA modeling)

#### SEO & Content Marketing

## Free vs Pro Split

### Free (browser tools, no signup)

ALL planning and generation tools:
- Campaign planner, content calendar, persona builder
- Ad copy generator, headline tester, subject line tester
- SEO audit, keyword suggestions, meta generator
- Survey builder, journey mapper, funnel visualizer
- UTM generator, ROI calculator, sample size calculator
- Social post generator, hashtag research, bio generator

### Pro ($9/mo — execution + data)

Tools that need real data or API connections:
- Actually post to X/LinkedIn/Instagram/Facebook (OAuth)
- Real analytics from connected accounts
- A/B test runner (hosts landing pages, tracks results)
- Email sending (Mailgun/SES integration)
- Scheduled posting with cron
- Autonomous campaign agent (AI generates → schedules → measures → adapts)
- Team workspaces (agency model — manage multiple clients)
- White-label reports (your branding on client reports)
